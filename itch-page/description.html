Make Your Past Self Useful

Chronost is a ten-level time-loop puzzle platformer about turning failed runs into part of the solution.

Every room gives you a short countdown. When time runs out—or you end the run early—your movements return as a ghost. Record the right path, let that ghost hold a switch or cross the room, then use the next timeline to push farther toward the exit.

The puzzles grow from one switch and one door into compact networks of platforms, gates, and overlapping routes. Later levels let you freeze a replay into a solid platform, while the hearts on screen limit how many timelines you can spend before the room resets.

Play in Normal Mode for harmless helpers, or enable Haunting Mode and make every collision with a past self dangerous.

Made for GMTK Game Jam 2026 and its theme, COUNT DOWN.

Features

  • Ten hand-built, single-screen puzzles
  • Record-and-replay ghosts that interact with switches and doors
  • Solid frozen ghosts that become temporary platforms
  • Normal and Haunting difficulty modes
  • A level browser with locally saved progress

Controls

  • A / D or Left / Right: Move
  • Space, W, or Up: Jump
  • S or Down: Drop through thin platforms
  • R: End the current loop and create a ghost
  • C: Reset the timeline and clear all ghosts
  • F: Create a ghost that freezes solid after replaying (from level 6)
  • B: Return to level selection
  • Esc: Pause

Browser Notes

Click the game once to give it keyboard focus. Basic touch controls for moving, jumping, looping, and resetting appear automatically on touch devices; a keyboard provides the complete control set.

Level progress and music settings are saved locally on your device.

If a browser extension captures single-key shortcuts, disable its bindings for this page while playing.
